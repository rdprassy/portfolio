# 60-Second Portfolio Introduction

This script is ready for an authentic camera or voice recording. It intentionally avoids a synthetic voice so the finished video communicates the person behind the portfolio.

## Spoken script

Hello, I am Devi Prasad Choudhary Ratnala, known online as rdprassy.

I am a full-stack engineer with more than ten years of experience building enterprise products across Java, Spring Boot, React, cloud platforms, data systems, and production operations.

My current focus is applied AI engineering: retrieval-augmented generation, multi-agent workflows, and analytics systems that help people make better decisions without losing traceability or human control.

What I bring is not only a list of technologies. I connect user workflows to architecture, data, failure modes, delivery, and measurable outcomes. I have built systems for cloud maintenance, operational insight, enterprise analytics, insurance, and platform services, and I have owned high-severity incidents through root-cause resolution.

Outside work, competitive programming, writing, and sport continue to shape how I think and collaborate.

If your team is solving a difficult product or engineering problem, I would be glad to compare notes.

## Shot plan

1. 0-8 seconds: Camera introduction with name and "rdprassy" title.
2. 8-20 seconds: Screen capture of the portfolio and selected project list.
3. 20-36 seconds: AI case-study architecture and retrieval-lab interaction.
4. 36-48 seconds: Experience timeline, GitHub, and incident-ownership proof.
5. 48-56 seconds: Sports, writing, or competitive-programming imagery.
6. 56-60 seconds: Camera close with website and contact call to action.

## Recording notes

- Use a quiet room, eye-level camera, and soft front lighting.
- Record horizontally at 1080p.
- Speak at a conversational pace and keep the result between 50 and 70 seconds.
- Add captions and remove background noise.
- Do not show confidential screens, client names, internal metrics, or personal phone numbers.

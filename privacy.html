Plain-language policy

Useful measurement without unnecessary collection.

This portfolio uses a consent-aware analytics design. Optional measurement is limited to understanding which public work is useful and where the experience can improve.

01

What may be measured

Page visits, coarse scroll depth and engagement, section visibility, project-filter categories, résumé views and downloads, portfolio-guide topics, command-palette destinations, media and live-product opens, contact clicks, and coarse page-load performance.

02

What is deliberately excluded

AI lab or portfolio-guide query text, project-search text, task titles, task notes, due dates, email or form content, phone numbers, document contents, advertising profiles, and cross-site personalization signals are not sent by the portfolio event layer. Only matched guide topics and search usage—not typed wording—may be measured. Task Matrix content remains in your browser.

03

Your choice

Google Analytics loads only after consent. Declining leaves the site fully functional. When analytics is active, the footer provides an “Analytics choices” control so the decision can be changed later.

04

How the data is used

Aggregate reporting helps improve navigation, content, résumé relevance, project presentation, and performance. It is not used to build advertising audiences.

05

YouTube media

Embedded players use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain and are not loaded until you choose a playlist. After loading a player, YouTube may receive device, network, cookie, and playback information under Google’s policies.

Current configuration

The site uses Google Analytics 4 after visitor consent. Advertising storage and personalization remain disabled, while the retired Universal Analytics integration has been removed.

Questions about portfolio data?

Contact Devi Prasad directly for clarification or a deletion request related to information you knowingly shared.
